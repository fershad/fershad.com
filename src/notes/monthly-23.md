## November 2025

🇯🇵 **TPAC 2025 in Kobe, Japan**. Having spent 3 weeks in Japan through October, I was back again in the second week of November for the W3C's annual TPAC gathering. It was a terrific experience, though was completely draining at the same time. Besides all the peopleing at the conference itself, I had a pretty decent shopping list from Kay of all the things we'd held off on buying on our holiday to Japan which I was supposed to pick up this time around. I knew the supermarkets & shopping streets of Kobe's Sannomiya area pretty well by the end of the week.

I've written more about my time at TPAC in [Reflections on TPAC 2025](/writing/reflections-tpac-2025/).

🏉 **Coaching Youth Touch in Taipei**. November also saw the start of the Touch season at the local American school here in Taipei. The girls at the school play Touch, while the boys play Rugby from November through to the end of January. This year I'm back coaching the Junior Varsity team, a challenging task given the wide skill range of the players in the squad, but one that is always rewarding as players develop through the course of the short season.

This year, for the first time, our team also has an end of season tournament which we are training towards! Exciting stuff for all involved.
